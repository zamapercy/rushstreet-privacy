About this policy

This policy explains how RUSH STREET handles information when you play the Android game or its browser version. It covers game progress, settings, advertising, local multiplayer, optional race recording and messages you send to the developer.

Game progress and settings

The game stores progress on your device: money, owned and selected cars, upgrades, completed events, race statistics and settings such as graphics, sound and driving controls. The Android app uses app-local storage; the browser version uses browser storage. The current game does not require an account or upload your solo save to a developer-operated cloud-save service.

If you choose to export or import a save backup, the game accesses the file you select through your device’s file picker. Exported backups remain wherever you save or share them.

Advertising and consent

The Android app includes Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform. Advertising services may process information including IP address and approximate location inferred from it, device or advertising identifiers, device and app information, ad impressions and interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. These services use information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, maintain security and improve their services. Ad availability and the information processed depend on your device, consent choices, region and the advertising configuration.

The game supports optional rewarded ads and interstitial ads between activities. Reward eligibility and ad limits are stored locally. Where applicable, Google’s consent form is presented before ads can be requested. You can use Ad Privacy Options in the game’s Settings when available, and your Android advertising privacy controls, to manage supported choices. Muting game audio does not change advertising consent.

Read Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s advertising information for details about Google’s processing and privacy controls. The browser game does not use the native AdMob integration.

Local multiplayer

Local multiplayer connects players on the same Wi-Fi network or hotspot. It exchanges a chosen driver name, selected car and upgrade information, steering and other driving inputs, car positions, race progress and results. The host receives connection information, including participants’ local network addresses, and controls the race simulation. Other participants can see driver names, cars and race activity.

This information is exchanged directly between connected devices, not through a developer-operated matchmaking server. Room codes help players join a room; use a network and fellow players you trust. Local multiplayer does not require a RUSH STREET account.

Driving sensors and network access

If you select tilt controls, device motion or orientation readings are used locally to calculate steering. The game does not use them to determine your physical location. Steering inputs may be exchanged with the host during local multiplayer.

An internet connection is required to open and use the current game. The app checks network availability, and advertising services may connect to Google. An internet requirement does not mean that solo progress is uploaded to the developer.

Optional race recording

Recording starts only when you choose it. On Android, screen recording requires the system’s screen-capture confirmation. The current native recorder captures screen video without microphone audio. The browser recorder captures the game canvas and may include the game’s audio mix. Neither recorder requests microphone or camera access.

Recordings are stored or downloaded on your device. On supported Android versions, completed recordings are saved under Movies / RUSH STREET; if automatic saving is unavailable, the app offers a file picker for export. The game does not automatically upload or share recordings. Content you record or export may include visible driver names and race activity.

Information you send us

If you email the developer for support or a privacy request, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, investigate the issue and handle the request. Please avoid sending passwords or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Sharing and retention

The game does not send solo save files or recordings to the developer automatically. Information is shared with local multiplayer participants as described above and with Google’s advertising services when those services are used. Information you voluntarily send by email may be processed by the email service used to receive and answer it. We may retain or disclose information we hold when necessary to comply with law or protect safety and legal rights.

Local saves remain until you reset game progress, clear the app or browser storage, or uninstall the app. Exported backups and recordings may remain after uninstalling and must be deleted separately from their saved locations. Support messages are retained as needed to resolve requests and meet applicable legal obligations. Google determines retention for information it processes under its own policies.

Your choices and requests

  • Manage ad consent through available in-game Ad Privacy Options and Android privacy settings.
  • Choose arrow controls instead of tilt controls.
  • Choose whether to use local multiplayer or create recordings and backup files.
  • Delete local progress by resetting it or clearing the app’s storage, and delete exported files separately.
  • Email zamantungwapercival@gmail.com to ask about information you have sent us or request its correction or deletion. Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights. We may need information to verify a request before acting on it.

The developer cannot directly delete information held independently by Google; Google’s privacy controls and request processes apply to that information.

Children’s privacy

The game does not ask children to submit personal details or create an account. If you believe a child has sent personal information to the developer, contact the email above so that we can review and address it. Advertising and consent must follow the age and regional requirements applicable to the game’s distribution.

Security and changes

We rely on device-provided app protections and take reasonable steps to protect information we handle, but no storage or communication method can be guaranteed completely secure. This policy webpage contains no developer-added advertising, analytics or contact form; its hosting provider may process standard technical request information to serve and protect the page.

We may update this policy when the game or its data practices change. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised date. For questions, contact Zamantungwa at zamantungwapercival@gmail.com.
